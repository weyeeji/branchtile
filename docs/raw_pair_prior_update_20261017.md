# Native-preserving pairing: additional 2026 prior-art check

The current result is an operator correctness gate, not a paper-ready claim. Sixteen actual BF16 capture states (two documents, two TP ranks, four layers) and four synthetic states pass integer-view bitwise comparison to native XQA, including full private tails. Whole-model qualification and complete-call timing remain pending.

[CoDec v2](https://arxiv.org/html/2505.17694v2), dated 28 March 2026, is the renamed FlashForge paper. Its §4 describes shared-prefix access, streaming softmax and tree reduction; §5 adds cost estimation and task scheduling. These already cover broad shared-prefix kernel and scheduling claims. Its reported attention and end-to-end TPOT gains use its own baselines and workloads, so those numbers cannot establish a target gain for our current vLLM/XQA workload. Any proposed contribution must distinguish native floating-point schedule preservation from generic prefix reuse.

The [public source](https://github.com/wzbxpy/codec) was inspected at `f70686e1024ea07f559be84d9096604293636149` in ignored `build/codec_prior`. The CUDA dispatch in `csrc/tree_attn.cpp` supports Hq32/Hkv8; our actual per-rank state is Hq16/Hkv2. `csrc/include/config.hpp` selects an F16 MMA, and `stage1_kernel.hpp` explicitly reinterprets inputs as `half` and uses half accumulators. Passing our BF16 payload unchanged would therefore be invalid. Casting or changing dispatch would create a port with a different numerical contract; it must be labeled and separately qualified, not represented as an unmodified paper baseline. No comparison timing has been run. Native XQA and existing mature FlashInfer cascade remain directly usable baselines.

[HARTS](https://arxiv.org/html/2608.28158v1), dated 28 August 2026, also constrains the novelty claim. It preserves chunkwise partitioning through bounded state replay for hybrid-attention training on rollout trees. Its numerical assessment is relative to baseline rerun variation. Our target is dense decode and exact native XQA arithmetic on a fixed launch schedule, but “preserving numerical paths while sharing prefixes” alone is too broad to claim as new.

Together with the already recorded ChunkAttention, Vosti and Taming comparisons, these findings restrict the candidate contribution to a concrete native-kernel state interface: split-local strided tile order, per-PV-warp fragments, backward row-max hints, original casts and original merge. Novelty still needs careful comparison and useful whole-model measurements. We do not claim general batch invariance, a formal determinism guarantee, or the first raw-state continuation method.
